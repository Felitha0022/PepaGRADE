ABOUT PepaGRADE

Supporting better assessment for Academic Papers (Essays, Literature Reviews, Proposals).

PepaGRADE provides a structured environment for academic assessment by combining artificial intelligence with lecturer-defined marking guides and assessment criteria.

The system is designed to assist lecturers in reviewing student academic writing while keeping the lecturer in control of the final assessment and feedback.

01

AI-Assisted

Supports the assessment process through AI-assisted academic writing analysis.

02

Guide-Based

Assessment is structured around lecturer-provided marking guides and criteria.

03

Lecturer Controlled

Lecturers review the assessment and remain responsible for the final academic decision.

THE PepaGRADE PLATFORM

Built around the academic assessment process.

01

AI-Powered Assessment

02

03
