Academic Writing Support

PepaGRADE is a web-based, AI-powered Academic Writing Feedback Assistant developed for Divine Word University.

The system supports the academic assessment process by providing structured feedback on student academic writing based on lecturer-provided assessment criteria.

Students can use the portal to access their enrolled course units, view assignments, submit academic papers and view grades released by their lecturers.
